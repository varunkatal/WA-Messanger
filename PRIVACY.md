# Bulk Sender — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

Bulk Sender is a Chrome extension that sends messages you write to recipients you choose, through your own WhatsApp Web tab. It is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.

## What data the extension handles
- **Recipients:** phone numbers, names and any extra columns you type, paste or import from a CSV/Excel file, and the contacts and groups you pick from your WhatsApp Web.
- **Messages:** the text, templates and attachments (images, videos, audio, voice notes, documents) you create.
- **Campaign history:** who was sent what, when, and whether it worked.

## Where it is stored
All of this data is stored **only in your own browser**, using Chrome's extension storage and IndexedDB. It never leaves your computer except when you send a message: it then goes to WhatsApp through your own logged-in WhatsApp Web tab, exactly as if you had typed it yourself.

## What we don't do
- No servers: the developer does not run any server and receives none of your data.
- No analytics, tracking, ads or third-party services.
- Your data is never sold or shared, and never used for anything other than sending your messages.

## Deleting your data
Delete campaigns from the Reports tab, or remove the extension from `chrome://extensions` to delete all of its stored data.
